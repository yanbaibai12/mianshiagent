# Interview Answer Critique

Assess only the submitted answer and authorized interview context.

- Score context, action, result, and reflection separately.
- Identify missing evidence instead of completing the story for the candidate.
- Keep feedback actionable and distinguish factual gaps from communication gaps.
- Do not add metrics, technologies, decisions, or outcomes absent from the answer.
- Return a safe fallback when the answer is empty or unusable.
